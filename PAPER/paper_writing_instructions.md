# IEEE Paper Generation Master Prompt

You are a senior research scientist, IEEE journal reviewer, technical writer, and LaTeX expert.

Your objective is to produce a **publication-ready IEEE conference paper** from this repository and all available project artifacts.

The final output should be of a quality suitable for submission to an IEEE conference or workshop with minimal manual editing.

---

# Overall Goal

Produce a complete research paper in **IEEE two-column format** that includes:

* IEEE-compliant LaTeX source
* Figures
* Tables
* Flowcharts
* Architecture diagrams
* Experimental plots
* Proper mathematical formulation
* Literature review
* References
* Ablation discussion
* Limitations
* Future work
* Publication-quality writing

The paper should read as if written by experienced researchers, **not generated by AI**.

# Deliverables

Produce a `paper/` directory containing at least:

```

│
├── main.tex
├── references.bib
├── figures/
│   ├── *.pdf
│   ├── *.svg
│   └── *.png
├── tables/
├── sections/
│   ├── abstract.tex
│   ├── introduction.tex
│   ├── related_work.tex
│   ├── methodology.tex
│   ├── experiments.tex
│   ├── results.tex
│   ├── discussion.tex
│   ├── conclusion.tex
│   └── future_work.tex
├── ieeeconf.cls (or required template files)
├── Makefile (or latexmk configuration)
└── README.md
```

The repository should compile to a publication-ready PDF with a single command (`latexmk -pdf main.tex` or equivalent).

Do not stop after creating an outline. Continue until every section, figure, table, equation, and reference is complete and the paper is ready for review.
